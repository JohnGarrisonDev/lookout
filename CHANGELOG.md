# Changelog

## 1.3.0 — 2026-11-26

- See your finished field: the solved card has an eye button that tucks it away so the whole field shows, confetti and all. Tap "Show results" (or the field) to bring the card back.
- The little "sees now" number on each lookout is bigger and bolder on small screens, so it reads on a 9x9 field on a phone.
- Journey levels you have solved now show your best time on their button. Something to beat.
- Times agree everywhere: the solved card, your best time and the shared result all count whole seconds the same way.
- Copy result: if your browser will not copy, the result appears in a box, selected, ready to copy by hand. The shared link is now the full https://games.oddvale.com/lookout/.
- "Show results" is a full-size button and no longer sits on top of the tools.

## 1.2.0 — 2026-11-26

- Plays better on phones: full screen on every phone, iPhone included, with a "Play full screen" button under the game.
- Painting with a finger is steadier: a slight wobble no longer spills into the next row, a ring shows the tile under your finger, and a tap just outside the field's edge still lands on the edge tile.
- Bigger buttons on touch screens, and a gentle buzz when you plant a hedge, when hedges touch and when you solve (Android, with sound on).
- The solved card and today's vote fit a small phone screen, upright or sideways, and the side panel's text is larger on a phone held sideways.

Newest first. Written for players.

## 1.1.0 (2026-11-25)

- After each of today's fields, tell us how hard it was with one tap, and see how your time compares with everyone else's today. Anonymous: nothing about you is stored.
- A new "Tell us" card under the game: a thumbs up or down and a few one-tap notes (too hard, too easy, broke on my phone...), no account needed.
- A Privacy link in the footer.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-24)

First release.

- **Daily:** three new fields every day, the same for everyone: Small, Medium and Large. Solve Small to keep your streak. Past 14 days stay playable (no streak credit), and "Copy result" gives you a spoiler-free line to share.
- **Journey:** 40 levels in 4 chapters. Each chapter teaches one more way of reasoning, from "this lookout already sees enough" to weighing every way a lookout could get its number.
- **Free play:** any size from 4x4 to 9x9, in Gentle, Medium, Hard or Fiendish.
- Every field has exactly one answer and never needs a guess.
- **Hint** explains the next logical step in a sentence and shows the tiles it is about. Press it again and it does the step. **Check** crosses out marks that do not fit.
- Lookouts show what they see right now, turn green with a tick when it is exactly right, and frown when it can no longer work. Touching hedges and cut-off patches are flagged as you go.
- Tap, long-press, right-click, drag to paint, or play entirely on the keyboard. Undo and redo. Your field is saved as you go.
